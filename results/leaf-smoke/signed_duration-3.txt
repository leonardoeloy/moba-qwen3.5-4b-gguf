I see the issue. The function `duration_minutes` calculates `sign * hours * 60 + minutes`, which means for negative durations with nonzero minutes, the sign only applies to the hours part, not the minutes.

For example:
- `duration_minutes(-1, 0, 30)` returns `-0 * 60 + 30 = 30` (should be `-30`)
- `duration_minutes(-1, 1, 30)` returns `-1 * 60 + 30 = -30` (correct)

The fix should be `sign * (hours * 60 + minutes)` to apply the sign to the entire magnitude.
